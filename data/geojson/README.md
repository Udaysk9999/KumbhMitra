# GeoJSON Data Directory

Stores geographic vectors, shapes, boundaries, and spatial corridors formatted in RFC 7946 GeoJSON.

### Planned Spatial Layers:
- `administrative-zones.geojson`: Sector divisions of Nashik and Trimbakeshwar for crowd management.
- `shahi-snan-routes.geojson`: Designated procession corridors for Akharas to Ramkund and Kushavarta.
- `pedestrian-zones.geojson`: Vehicle-free walking zones during peak bathing dates.
- `shuttle-corridors.geojson`: Ring road public shuttle loops and emergency vehicle lanes.
- `godavari-riverbank.geojson`: River line and ghat access stairways.
